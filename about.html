About TOS

Technology focused. Problem driven. Responsibly delivered.

Technology Oriented Solutions is an independent South African software and cybersecurity solutions studio based in Cape Town.

Technology shaped around the problem

TOS designs practical digital systems for organisations that need clearer workflows, secure information handling and dependable customer experiences. Our work includes business applications, booking platforms, healthcare workflows, ecommerce foundations, cloud deployment and technical support.

Responsible development

Demonstrations use fictional or explicitly approved test data. Systems intended for production must complete client-specific testing, privacy, security, backup, recovery and acceptance checks.

Founder-led delivery

TOS is currently founder-led. This keeps communication direct and ensures that discovery, implementation and support remain closely connected to the business problem being solved.

Operating principles

  • Understand the workflow before selecting technology.
  • Protect sensitive information by design.
  • Demonstrate value before broad implementation.
  • Document systems for maintainability and support.
  • Be honest about prototype and production status.
