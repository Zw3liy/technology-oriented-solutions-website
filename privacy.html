This notice explains how Technology Oriented Solutions (TOS) handles information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

The contact form may collect your name, email address, organisation and the message you submit. Please do not submit passwords, patient records, financial credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, understand a requested service, arrange demonstrations and maintain reasonable business records.

Service providers

This website is hosted by Netlify. Form submissions may be processed and stored by Netlify and forwarded to the TOS business mailbox. Service providers may process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy documentation.

Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, legitimate business records or applicable legal requirements. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable obligations and lawful exceptions.

Contact

Privacy enquiries can be sent to technologyorientedsolutions@proton.me.
